- **Vent Hopping:** In ventilation sections, maintain mini form but continuously bunny-hop. The lowered ceiling ceiling-glitches small models slightly forward, producing a minor speed boost.
- **Camera Angling:** Keep your third-person camera angled directly top-down whenever navigating narrow beams. When grown, your avatar's shoulders block forward sightlines, making top-down perspective essential for accurate foot placement.
