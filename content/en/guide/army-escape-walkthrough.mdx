## Phase 2: Armory and Communications Center

Stepping into the armory shifts the gameplay from simple object searching to intricate logical puzzles. ### Armory Weapon Crate Cipher

The central weapons crate requires a color-wheel input determined by the rank insignias hanging along the wall. Player experience shows that standard military insignia—ranging from Private stripes to General stars—correspond to numerical counts:

*   **Single Chevron:** Red dial = 1
*   **Double Chevrons:** Green dial = 2
*   **Triple Chevrons with Arc:** Yellow dial = 4
*   **Five-Pointed Star:** Blue dial = 5

Entering the sequence `1 - 2 - 4 - 5` opens the locking mechanism, granting you access to the **Blowtorch Handle** and an uncharged battery pack.

### Communications Terminal & Fuse Box Alignment

Head toward the radio console on the right side of the armory. The monitor displays intermittent static caused by three blown fuses inside the auxiliary power panel. 

| Fuse Socket | Required Amperage | Found Location | Function Activated |

The communications monitor will light up, displaying coordinates `38°N - 77°W`, which serves as the electronic keypad password (`3877`) for the Armory Exit Hatch.

1. Collect the **Empty Oil Can** resting beside the workbench tire stack.
2. Approach the fuel drum near the APC vehicle and use the oil can to fill it with hydraulic fluid.
3. Lubricate the rusted gear shaft located behind the vehicle lift console.
4. Insert the **Iron Gear** (recovered from the tool chest) into the motorized gear assembly.
5. Pull the primary lever downward. The platform elevates, creating a ramp up to the ventilation walkway.

You have reached the final perimeter boundary. Escaping this final military cordon requires disabling the searchlight matrix and triggering the automated gate motors.

### Searchlight Matrix Disablement

The control booth overlooks the electric perimeter fence. Two automated spotlights continuously sweep across the main gate. Entering their line of sight alerts the guard system, resetting the gate locks.

To disable the spotlights:
1. Access the roof ladder using the **Blowtorch Handle** combined with the **Gas Cylinder** found near the motor pool workbench to melt the padlock.
2. Open the spotlight junction box. You will encounter a wire-cutting puzzle.
3. Refer to the electrical diagram discovered on the communications board in Sector 2. Cut the **Striped Yellow Wire** first, followed by the **Solid Blue Wire**. Do not touch the red live wire.

Once the electricity to the fence is cut, interact with the heavy steel portcullis terminal. Combine the **Torn Map Fragment (Part 1)** from the barracks with **Torn Map Fragment (Part 2)** taken from the watchtower desk drawer.

Select the terminal screen, convert the military phonetic letters to their keypad equivalents (B=2, D=4), and punch in the numeric sequence `2-9-4-4`. The steel barriers retract, completing your escape from the base!

### What should I do if an item in my inventory cannot be combined?
Most military escape games require items to be combined in a strict order of operations. Inspect base items in detailed view first, ensure all auxiliary parts (such as washers, screws, or batteries) are equipped, and confirm that both items belong to the same puzzle subsystem.

### Where can I find the second map piece in this army escape walkthrough?
The second map fragment is locked inside the top-right drawer of the watchtower radio desk. You must use the magnetic key fob dropped by the locker latch in the Sector 3 motor pool to open this specific magnetic drawer.

### Why does the alarm sound when I cut the perimeter fence wires?
The alarm triggers if the secondary auxiliary generator is still supplying power to the perimeter fence. You must disable the primary transformer switchboard inside the watchtower control booth before cutting any physical fence wires with your pliers.
