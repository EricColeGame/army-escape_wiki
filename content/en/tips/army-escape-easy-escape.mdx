## Troubleshooting Alarms: How to Reset the Alert State

Even veteran players make mistakes. A single misjudged jump or errant footstep can trigger an amber alert, sending guards into investigative modes. Knowing how to de-escalate is vital to preserving your run.

*   **Do Not Panic Sprint:** Running generates heavy audio signatures that pull nearby guards directly to your trajectory.
*   **Break Line of Sight Twice:** Turn two 90-degree corners immediately. Breaking line of sight once only causes guards to check your last known position; the second turn puts you out of their sweeping cone.
*   **Go Vertical or Sub-Surface:** Guards prioritize sweeping ground levels. Climbing onto warehouse rafters, duct systems, or dropping into drainage gutters pulls you outside standard NPC horizontal cones.
*   **Wait Out the Search Timer:** Base alert timers generally step down from Red (Engaged) to Amber (Searching) in 30 seconds, then return to White (Idle) after 60 seconds if no further cues occur.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes an army escape easy escape path fail most often?
The primary reason an **army escape easy escape** fails is rushing past perimeter lights without accounting for secondary guard sightlines. Players often watch the moving searchlight but fail to notice static tower snipers or roving canine units in the shadows.

### Do wire cutters make noise during perimeter breaches?
Standard wire cutters generate very low audio, but snaring the fence mesh causes metallic vibration sounds. To ensure silence, cut the bottom links rather than the center, and ensure no patrol is within 10 meters of the fence section.

### How do I handle guard dogs during an army escape?
K9 units rely on scent trails and audio rather than visual identification. Crossing shallow waterways, wading through drainage culverts, or deploying pepper-based deterrent sprays disrupts their tracking ability, allowing you to bypass them without firing a shot.
