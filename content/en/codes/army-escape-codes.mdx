## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are army escape codes and what do they provide?
These promotional alphanumeric vouchers are released by the game developers to reward players with free items. Claiming them grants in-game gold, barracks EXP, crafting components like Ancient Fragments, and temporary booster potions that increase luck and item drop rates.

### How often are new codes released?
New vouchers typically appear when the game crosses specific engagement goals, such as like counts, player favorite milestones, or when substantial balance patches and seasonal holidays arrive.

### Do active potion bonuses stack?
In most cases, potions of the same type extend the duration rather than multiplying the intensity (e.g., using two 20-minute Luck Potions yields 40 minutes of double luck). However, distinct potion types—such as combining a Gold Potion with a Luck Potion—run simultaneously without issue.

### Why did my code say it succeeded, but I cannot find my items?
Certain materials, such as Minotaur Horns, World Eater Eyes, or Ancient Fragments, bypass your primary gold balance and deposit directly into your crafting components bag. Open your main backpack inventory tab to view your updated stock.
